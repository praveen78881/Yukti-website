FAQ

Questions CAs ask us.

Can I bring my existing data from Tally or Excel?

Yes. Import opening balances and masters from Excel, and bank statements as CSV from any bank. A Tally XML importer is on the roadmap for the next release.

Which ITR forms does Yukti support?

ITR-1 through ITR-4 at launch, with the form chosen automatically from the entity type. Schedules are pre-filled from the P&L and balance sheet.

Does it work without internet?

Yukti is offline-first. Pass entries without a connection and everything syncs when you're back online.

How many clients can I manage?

Starter covers one company. Practice covers 25, and the Firm plan has no limit. Switch between companies from the sidebar.

Is my clients' data safe?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored on servers in India, and never shared with third parties.
